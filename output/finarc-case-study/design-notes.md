# Finarc product story

The existing master has been refined in place. It contains 27 sections across five chapters, with 13 editable UI spaces. The canvas is 1440 × 30270.

Typography now uses Inter throughout. Display titles use Medium, 98 percent line height, and tight tracking. Body copy stays readable with quieter contrast. The layout combines black, white, and pale neutral sections, bento compositions, broad desktop screen spaces, and portrait mobile screen spaces.

The narrative opens with the promise and human need, then presents the product, ownership, and the thinking behind it. Illustrative amounts and proposed validation targets remain labeled. Product scope is grounded in the project implementation.
